# The OIKOS Manifesto

We share one planet and one finite set of resources.

We believe that how those resources move should be **visible**, **computable** and **open to challenge**
by every person it affects.

We believe that intelligence applied to allocation must be **open**: open code, open models, open
data, open decisions. A system that cannot be inspected cannot be trusted, and a system that cannot
be contested cannot be just.

We believe that essential medicine should not be withheld by anything other than the limits of
physical production, and that solving this requires honest work on safety, quality and the funding
of future research.

We believe that artificial intelligence should **serve** human decisions, never replace them.
Every automated proposal must be explainable, reversible and subject to open governance.

We do not claim to have solved this. We claim that the problem is worth building in public.

**Build it open. Build it careful. Build it for everyone.**
